Examples

Queries

Angle brackets pull a random word from the dictionary. Filters and inflections can be written with a space, dash, or dot. 

<firstname male>
<noun-animal plural>
<verb.ed>
<pron poss male>

Blocks

Curly braces choose an alternative. Repeaters and separators apply to the next block. A block with no repeater runs once. 

{heads|tails}
[rep:3][sep:\s]{click|clack}
{plain text works now}

Carriers & articles

Name a result so you can reuse it. [a] inserts a or an in front of the next word. 

<firstname male :: hero> saw <::hero>
[a] <noun animal>
